About Kimberly

Independent app developer, author, and digital creator — building tools and stories that uplift, encourage, and make a difference.

Hi, I'm Kimberly ♡

I'm the founder of The Gifted Girl Kim LLC, where I create mobile apps, books, and digital media with purpose. My work blends thoughtful design, privacy-first engineering, and a warm, faith-guided creative spirit.

From screenshot cleanup tools and nature discovery apps to Scripture companions and heartfelt books, every project starts with a simple question: How can this genuinely help someone?

I believe technology should feel human — beautiful, trustworthy, and built with care. Whether you're downloading an app, reading a book, shopping on my Etsy store, or reaching out for support, I want your experience to feel warm, respectful, and worth your time.
