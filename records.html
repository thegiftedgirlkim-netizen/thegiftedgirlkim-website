About GGK Records

GGK Records is the independent songwriting and original-music brand of The Gifted Girl Kim LLC. From Oklahoma, the label creates original storytelling songs shaped by family heritage, ranch life, faith, love, and personal experience.

Every composition in this collection is original creative work, written to carry a story.
