Support

Need help with an app? Start here — or email us directly and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Email Support

For app help, bug reports, or account questions, email thegiftedgirlkim@gmail.com and include your app name, device model, and iOS/Android version.

Screenshot Goblin

Screenshot Goblin is coming soon to the Apple App Store. It scans your photo library locally on your device — nothing is uploaded. If scan fails after launch, open Settings → Screenshot Goblin → Photos and allow access. Deleted screenshots go to Recently Deleted / Trash.

Digital Chapel

Digital Chapel is coming soon to the Apple App Store. It offers Scripture reading, narration, prayer features, and reflection tools. Some features may require an internet connection. For billing or subscription help, include your store receipt in your email.

Afterlife Network

Afterlife Network is live on the Apple App Store. It includes spirit communication tools with community safety controls. For help, include your device model and iOS version when you email support.

Mappy Navigator

Mappy Navigator is live on the Apple App Store. It helps you save journey spots and smart parking locations. Location permission is required for map features. If addresses look wrong, include the exact search term you used.

Meditation Journey-444

Meditation Journey-444 is live on the Apple App Store. For playback or session issues, force-close the app and reopen. Include your device model and whether you're on Wi‑Fi or cellular when you email support.

Memory Friend

Memory Friend is designed as a gentle personal companion app. Support requests about reminders, memories, or accessibility are welcome — tell us what you were trying to do.

SocialNature

SocialNature is live on the Apple App Store — a nature discovery app featuring Traveler the Turtle. For crashes or content issues, include screenshots if possible when you email support.

Dream Board

Dream Board combines vision boards, journaling, and goal tracking. If you're using an archived build, note the version shown in Settings when you contact support.

Mobile Eyes

Mobile Eyes is a caregiver safety prototype. Backend features may be limited during development — describe the feature you expected and what happened instead.

Snow Patrol

Snow Patrol is in active development. We'd love your feedback on gameplay, story moments, and performance on your device.

Gem & Me

Gem & Me is a companion app prototype. Share what interaction felt helpful or confusing — your feedback shapes the next version.
